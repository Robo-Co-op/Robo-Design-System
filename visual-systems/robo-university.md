# Robo University Visual System

**Learn Together — Explorative Digital Education**

Continuous learning is represented through paths, checkpoints, branching modules, and update states.

- Foundation: Mint Cream / Deep Forest
- Active learning: Fresh Lime
- Exploration: Energy Purple
- Achievement: Gold
- Core: clear, optimistic learning system
- Explore: darker, experimental learning-map mode

Avoid school clichés, childish gamification, and inaccessible lime-on-white combinations.
